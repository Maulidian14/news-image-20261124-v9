1 · Taiwan’s record rally

The Taiex leaps 8.99% to a record 40,122.35 — its biggest one-day point gain ever (+3,310) — as TSMC hits its limit-up price of NT$2,230. Tokyo’s Nikkei adds 3.91% to 58,644.63 and Seoul’s Kospi 6.32% to 5,006.03 on tariff relief.

E. ASIASources: CNA / Nikkei Asia

2 · Xi’s purge reaches Shanghai

Politburo member Chen Jining, Shanghai’s party chief, is under investigation for “serious violations of discipline and laws” — the fourth of 24 Politburo members probed since 2025, and the first sitting Shanghai chief investigated since 2006.

E. ASIASources: Reuters / Xinhua

3 · Houthis hit Riyadh’s tallest tower

A Houthi drone strikes the 80-storey PIF Tower in Riyadh’s financial district — the second hit in five days. Saudi Civil Defense says 11 people were wounded; the Saudi-led coalition vows a decisive response.

M. EASTSources: Reuters / AP

4 · Four tankers hit in Hormuz

Iran’s navy says four tankers struck mines in the Strait of Hormuz on Tuesday; Reuters reports at least two hit in the morning. The claims are not independently verified.

M. EASTSources: Reuters / AP

5 · Russian jets enter Korea’s air zone

South Korean fighters scramble as Russian military jets enter the Korea Air Defense Identification Zone; residents of Ulleungdo island are told to shelter indoors.

E. ASIASources: Yonhap / AP

6 · NATO sends AWACS to Poland

NATO authorises AWACS surveillance jets for Poland after last week’s missile incursion; a Russian drone briefly crosses into Polish airspace overnight and turns back east.

EUROPESources: Reuters / AFP

7 · Ukraine sinks Russian corvette

Ukrainian naval drones sink Russia’s corvette Merkury in Benghazi’s port, Libya; Italy voices concern over a strike in a harbour near NATO waters.

AFRICASources: AP / AFP

8 · Zaporizhzhia: 2 killed, 40 hurt

An overnight Russian drone attack kills two and wounds 40, including four children, hitting an apartment block, the regional governor says.

EUROPESources: Reuters / BBC

9 · Real Madrid rout PSV 5–1

EUROPESources: BBC Sport / ESPN

10 · Sanofi’s $41bn Insmed buyout

France’s Sanofi agrees to buy US biotech Insmed for $175 a share in cash — the biggest biotech deal in nearly three years — to bolster its respiratory pipeline.

EUROPESources: Bloomberg / Reuters

11 · Giuliani gets Medal of Freedom

Trump presents the nation’s highest civilian honour to the former New York mayor, 82, in a closed-press Oval Office ceremony — the third recipient of his second term.

N. AMERICASources: Fox News / AP

12 · Oxfam: Gaza toxic across 99%

Heavy metals, explosive residues, asbestos and dioxins contaminate air, soil and water across almost all of Gaza, Oxfam and CEOBS find.

M. EASTSources: AP / AFP

13 · NATO vows to defend space
